Our story

A dream that started with passion, growing into something special.

Hello! I'm Paba, the founder of Cake Wings.

For me, entrepreneurship has always been about more than just running a business. It's about following my passion, believing in my dreams and creating something that brings happiness to others.

My journey started in 2021 when I launched my clothing brand, VinteX. It was one of my first big steps into entrepreneurship, and it taught me so much about building a brand, understanding customers and turning an idea into something real. That experience gave me the confidence and motivation to dream bigger.

And that's how Cake Wings came into my life!

I wanted to create something different — something that would allow me to share happiness, make people smile and become part of their most special moments.

I have always loved the idea of making someone feel special through a thoughtful surprise. Think about the excitement of a birthday surprise, the happiness of receiving a beautiful cake, the emotions behind an anniversary gift or the unforgettable feeling of seeing a surprise arranged just for you. Those little moments mean so much, and I wanted to be the person who helps create them.

That's what Cake Wings is all about.

Today, we do much more than cakes. We create personalised birthday surprises, anniversary arrangements, romantic proposals, midnight surprises, office celebrations and even special vehicle surprises. From beautiful cakes and customised gifts to balloons, flowers and complete surprise arrangements, we put creativity and love into every detail.

We also welcome orders from Sri Lankans living overseas who want to make their loved ones back home feel special. Even if you're miles away, you can still make someone's day unforgettable. I'll help you bring that surprise to life!

As a 25-year-old female entrepreneur, I'm building this brand with so much happiness, passion and determination. Of course, every business journey comes with challenges, but every happy customer, every beautiful celebration and every smile makes the journey worthwhile.

My dream is to grow Cake Wings into a brand that people think of whenever they want to celebrate someone, express their love or create a beautiful memory.

I don't just want to deliver cakes or arrange surprises. I want people to remember how they felt when they received them.

Thank you for being part of my journey, trusting my little dream and allowing me to be part of your special moments. Every order means something to me, and I can't wait to create many more beautiful memories with you!

With love,

Paba

Founder – Cake Wings

About us

Every celebration deserves a little magic.

At Cake Wings, we believe that life's most beautiful moments deserve to be celebrated in unforgettable ways. Whether it's a birthday, anniversary, romantic proposal, midnight surprise or a special occasion, we turn your ideas into thoughtful experiences that create lasting memories.

We specialise in creating personalised surprises and celebration experiences, bringing together beautifully crafted cakes, stunning decorations, balloons, flowers, gifts and carefully planned details. From elegant birthday arrangements to exciting vehicle surprises and office celebrations, we make every occasion feel special.

We also help loved ones living overseas celebrate the people they care about in Sri Lanka. Distance should never stop you from making someone feel loved and appreciated. You choose the surprise, and we'll help bring it to life.

At Cake Wings, it's not just about the cake, the gifts or the decorations. It's about the smiles, the excitement and the beautiful memories created along the way.

Our passion is making ordinary moments extraordinary, one surprise at a time.
